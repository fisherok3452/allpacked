Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AllPacked handles information when you use the app.

Information you enter

AllPacked lets you create trips, packing lists, categories, and shopping items. The app uses the information you enter to provide these features.

Accounts

AllPacked does not require you to create an AllPacked account to use the app.

In-App Purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. AllPacked does not receive your full payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Personal information

We do not sell personal information. The current version of AllPacked is not designed to collect personal information through a developer-operated account system.

Diagnostics and platform services

Apple and other platform services may process technical, diagnostic, purchase, or device information according to their own policies and settings.

Children

AllPacked is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an AllPacked account system.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as AllPacked changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.
